1.8.0. They contain only the English and Polish words listed in manifest.json.
They are not recordings taken from songs or from a commercial voice service.

Reproduction: scripts/generate-voices.py in the Piano Practice repository pins
both model downloads and verifies their SHA-256 digests. Processing trims excess
edge silence, adds short fades, and normalizes the peak to -2 dBFS. Output format:
mono signed 16-bit PCM WAV, 22050 Hz. manifest.json records each resulting clip's
text, duration, format, level, and SHA-256 digest.
